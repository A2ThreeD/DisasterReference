# Source review

Initial public-source review on 2026-11-18. The table describes the material inspected and what it can contribute to the catalog. The [evidence CSV](../catalog/evidence.csv) records resource-level observations.

| Project | Public material inspected | How it informs DisasterReference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project N.O.M.A.D. | [Official repository and application inventory](https://github.com/Crosstalk-Solutions/project-nomad) | Shows the breadth of a Kiwix-based library alongside maps and education tools. Use it to discover reference collections and check coverage. The application inventory alone gives collection-level evidence. |
| Prepper Disk | [Public product contents](https://www.prepperdisk.com/) and [Top 5 Online Survival Preps](https://www.prepperdisk.com/pages/top-5-online-preps-list) | Its public list recommends keeping equipment-specific manuals, map coverage, medical resources, and other offline references. The iFixit recommendation is traceable to a named collection. A product category or abbreviated title needs an exact publisher match before it supports a document-level claim. |
| PrepperPi | [jmarler/prepperpi](https://github.com/jmarler/prepperpi) and its [Starter bundle manifest](https://github.com/jmarler/prepperpi/blob/main/bundles/builtin/manifests/starter.yaml) | The manifest identifies specific Kiwix collections. Its inclusion of iFixit is cataloged as a listing for the archive edition. This is a provisional match for the supplied name. [Offgrid Technology's PrepperPi](https://www.offgrid.technology/index.php/2026/04/20/introducing-prepperpi-your-offline-survival-library-on-a-raspberry-pi/) is another publicly documented project with the same name. |
| Solomon Survival Tech | [Solomon Drive overview](https://go.solomonsurvivaltech.com/) and [getting started guide](https://go.solomonsurvivaltech.com/getting-started/) | The public material shows the browse and search flow. An edition-specific file inventory remains open for review. |
| Survival Codex | [What's Inside](https://survivalcodex.com/pages/whats-inside) | The public preview puts urgent tasks such as finding safe water ahead of a deeper reference shelf, with related-entry links and e-ink layouts. The illustrative water preview attributes guidance to CDC without identifying an exact document edition. |
| Lastbook | [Official repository](https://github.com/Immortal-Protocols/lastbook), [water task](https://github.com/Immortal-Protocols/lastbook/blob/main/content/topics/water.json), [communications task](https://github.com/Immortal-Protocols/lastbook/blob/main/content/topics/comms.json), and [gap log](https://github.com/Immortal-Protocols/lastbook/blob/main/content/gaps.json) | The public task files link decisions to specific readings and pages. The water file explicitly points to FEMA P-530 for a particular earthquake scenario. Track similar page-level references and record uncovered tasks. |

For project overlap, follow shared dependencies back to the publisher. Two bundles that draw from the same Kiwix collection help confirm availability. Independent recommendations require independently authored editorial evidence for the same work.

The first pass is U.S.-oriented because its publisher set includes CDC, EPA, FEMA, NOAA, and USGS. Future passes should use equivalent authorities for other jurisdictions and tie local maps, alerts, and equipment manuals to the user's region and devices.

## Survival Codex's Wikipedia selection

The [product page](https://survivalcodex.com/), [press kit](https://survivalcodex.com/pages/press), and [comparison page](https://survivalcodex.com/pages/offline-survival-library-compared) describe more than 16,000 text-only Wikipedia articles on medicine, farming, engineering, chemistry, and other survival and rebuilding topics. The public pages inspected did not provide a title list, revision snapshot, inclusion rules, or a reproducible build method. The public [manual library](https://survivalcodex.com/pages/library) names documents individually, including Army map reading and field sanitation manuals.

DisasterReference's [Wikipedia selection](../catalog/wikipedia.csv) starts with two articles per category and records the use case for each. For a broader offline library, [Kiwix describes `nopic`](https://get.kiwix.org/en/faq-items/what-do-mini-nopic-and-maxi-mean-in-the-wikipedia-zim-files/) as full article text without images. The archive format and the editorial choice of which articles to include are separate decisions.

## Preparing beforehand

Emergency management offices and university guidance converge on keeping a supply kit and writing a household plan. The [City of Denton](https://www.cityofdenton.com/462/Plan-to-Build-a-Supply-Kit) and the [Building America Solution Center](https://basc.pnnl.gov/library/readygov-build-kit) point to the exact FEMA Ready.gov kit page. Direct access returned an error during review, so the download manifest includes a December 2024 Kiwix snapshot fallback. The [American Red Cross kit checklist](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/survival-kit-supplies.html) was checked at the publisher. [Cornell Emergency Management](https://emergency.cornell.edu/preparedness/build-a-kit/) independently supports the household kit task.

[FEMA P-2064](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-HS5_100-PURL-gpo159555) is a broader handbook. The [Building America Solution Center](https://basc.pnnl.gov/library/are-you-ready-depth-guide-citizen-preparedness-fema-p-2064) catalogs it and [Santa Cruz County](https://www.santacruzcountyca.gov/Portals/0/County/CAO/pdfs/PreparednessInfoEnglish/are-you-ready-guide.pdf) provides an access copy. The [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/disasters-and-emergencies/get-prepared-before-disaster-emergency-strikes/) and the [El Paso Office of Emergency Management](https://elpasoready.org/financial-preparedness/) both specifically recommend completing or downloading the named Emergency Financial First Aid Kit. The resource catalog retains the formal candidate status pending maintainer review.
