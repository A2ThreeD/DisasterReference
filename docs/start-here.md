# First readings for an emergency library

This starter list covers all eight PrepperCache-aligned categories with English-language, primarily U.S. sources. Use the [recommended download manifest](../catalog/recommended.csv) to save the starter documents. Historical references are labeled in the guide. [Maintainer review](reviewing.md) records which documents have been approved, revised, or removed.

## Prepare Beforehand

Start with a household kit and a plan for evacuation, sheltering, and staying in touch. Save a broader handbook and keep completed contact or financial forms with your private emergency records.

| Task | Recommended reading |
| --- | --- |
| Build a household kit | [Ready.gov Build A Kit](https://www.ready.gov/kit) ([archived copy](https://kiwix.tader.nl/content/www.ready.gov_en_2024-12/www.ready.gov/kit)) and [Red Cross survival kit checklist](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/survival-kit-supplies.html) |
| Plan evacuation and household reunification | [Red Cross disaster preparedness plan](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/make-a-plan.html) and [FEMA family communication plan (PDF)](https://www.weather.gov/media/afc/Hydro/FloodSafety/family-communication-plan_fillable-card.pdf) |
| Organize emergency records and financial information | [Emergency Financial First Aid Kit (PDF)](https://operationhope.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/EFFAK.pdf) |
| Read a household preparedness handbook | [FEMA Are You Ready? (PDF)](https://www.santacruzcountyca.gov/Portals/0/County/CAO/pdfs/PreparednessInfoEnglish/are-you-ready-guide.pdf) |

## Emergency reference by category

### Medical & First Aid

- Assess someone who is hurt or ill. [Red Cross First Aid Steps](https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/first-aid/performing-first-aid/first-aid-steps)
- Find CPR instructions. [Red Cross CPR Steps](https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/cpr/performing-cpr/cpr-steps)
- Find guidance for severe bleeding. [Red Cross Life-Threatening External Bleeding](https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/learn-first-aid/bleeding-life-threatening-external)

**Army field references**

- Consult the Army's 2026 first-aid and casualty-care manual for field reference. [ATP 4-02.11 (PDF)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN46159-ATP_4-02.11-000-WEB-1.pdf) · [Survival Codex access copy](https://survivalcodex.com/cdn/shop/files/survival-codex-library-atp-4-02-11-casualty-response-tccc-first-aid_dcef2c2a-a4ee-4dac-b7b0-1df583182797.pdf?v=7408515831674510452)
- Find the relevant chapter by topic and PDF page. [Army first-aid page index](army-first-aid-pages.md)
- Look up the older joint-service first-aid manual for historical context. [FM 4-25.11 (PDF, 2002 with Change 1)](https://survivalcodex.com/cdn/shop/files/survival-codex-library-fm-4-25-11-first-aid.pdf?v=8583007396900567922)

### Food and Water

- Store emergency drinking water. [CDC emergency water supply guide](https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/about/how-to-create-and-store-an-emergency-water-supply.html)
- Find emergency drinking-water treatment guidance. [CDC emergency water fact sheet (PDF)](https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/media/pdfs/make-water-safe-during-emergency-p.pdf)
- Check refrigerated and frozen food after an outage. [FoodSafety.gov outage charts (PDF)](https://www.in.gov/localhealth/portercounty/files/Food-Safety-During-Power-Outage-_-FoodSafety.gov.pdf)
- Look up household hygiene when running water is disrupted. [CDC emergency personal hygiene](https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/safety/guidelines-for-personal-hygiene-during-an-emergency.html) · [Alternate CDC host](https://portugues.cdc.gov/water-emergency/safety/guidelines-for-personal-hygiene-during-an-emergency.html)
- Find tested home food preservation guidance. [USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning (PDF)](https://growappalachia.berea.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/USDA-Complete-Guide-to-Home-Canning-2015-revision-1.pdf)

### Survival & Shelter

- Decide where to go and how to reunite. [Red Cross disaster preparedness plan](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/make-a-plan.html)
- Prepare for extreme heat and find cooling options. [Red Cross Extreme Heat Safety](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/types-of-emergencies/extreme-heat-safety.html)
- Prepare for and shelter during winter storms. [Red Cross winter storm checklist (PDF)](https://www.redcross.org/content/dam/redcross/get-help/pdfs/winter-storm/EN_Winter-Storm-Preparedness-Checklist.pdf)

**Find or make shelter**

- **Core.** Choose a site and build a tarp, brush, debris, or snow shelter. [Building Wilderness Shelter (illustrated PDF)](../published/pdf/reference/survival-shelter/building-wilderness-shelter.pdf)
- Choose a safer site for an outdoor shelter. [NPS Finding and Setting Up a Campsite](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/camping/finding-and-setting-up-a-campsite.htm)
- Read the source pamphlet for more wilderness survival guidance. [Scouting America Wilderness Survival, shelter section (PDF)](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/Merit_Badge_ReqandRes/Pamphlets/Wilderness%20Survival.pdf#page=25)
- Keep a room warm during a heating outage. [Utah Emergency Management Shelter at Home in the Cold](https://dem.utah.gov/shelter-at-home-in-the-cold/)
- Find a shelter that is open now during an evacuation. [Red Cross live shelter locator](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/disaster-relief-and-recovery-services/find-an-open-shelter.html)

**Other weather hazards**

- Check flood warnings and flood safety. [NWS Flood Safety Tips and Resources](https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood) · [During a flood](https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-during)
- Find shelter guidance for a tornado threat. [NWS Tornado Safety](https://www.weather.gov/safety/tornado) · [During a tornado](https://www.weather.gov/safety/tornado-during)
- Find shelter guidance during thunderstorms. [NWS Lightning Safety Tips and Resources](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning) · [Lightning safety tips](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-tips)

### Repair

- Assess hazards before flood cleanup. [EPA Flooded Homes Cleanup Guidance](https://www.epa.gov/flooded-homes)
- Recognize and report a suspected gas pipeline leak. [PHMSA pipeline leak guide](https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/safety-awareness/pipeline/pipeline-leak-recognition-and-what-do) · [PHMSA public response companion](https://primis.phmsa.dot.gov/stakeholder-comms/emergencyresponse/)
- Identify electrical equipment that needs replacement or professional review after water damage. [ESFI Water-Damaged Electrical Equipment](https://www.esfi.org/water-damaged-electrical-equipment/) · [Water-damaged electrical equipment graphic (PDF)](https://www.esfi.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/ESFI-Submerged-Equipment-2025.pdf)
- Check mold cleanup guidance after a disaster. [Federal guide for homeowners and renters (EPA-hosted PDF)](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2017-08/documents/mold._homeowners_and_renters_guide_to_cleanup_after_disasters.pdf)

### Communication

- Make a printable household contact plan. [FEMA family communication plan (PDF)](https://www.weather.gov/media/afc/Hydro/FloodSafety/family-communication-plan_fillable-card.pdf)
- Find the local weather radio broadcast. [NOAA Weather Radio](https://www.weather.gov/nwr/) · [NOAA Weather Radio brochure (PDF)](https://www.weather.gov/media/pah/Skywarn/noaaweatherradiobrochure.pdf)
- Understand official emergency alerts on your phone. [National Weather Service Wireless Emergency Alerts](https://www.weather.gov/wrn/wea) · [NWS Wireless Emergency Alerts fact sheet (PDF)](https://www.weather.gov/media/wrn/WEA-factsheet-2021-final.pdf)

### Power and Energy

- Find guidance for hazards during a blackout. [CDC power outage guide](https://www.cdc.gov/natural-disasters/response/what-to-do-protect-yourself-during-a-power-outage.html) · [Alternate CDC host](https://portugues.cdc.gov/natural-disasters/response/what-to-do-protect-yourself-during-a-power-outage.html)
- Make a household plan for an outage and recovery. [Red Cross Power Outage Safety](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/types-of-emergencies/power-outage.html)
- Plan for medical equipment that needs electricity. [FDA home-use medical device booklet (PDF)](https://www.fda.gov/media/80782/download)
- Find carbon monoxide prevention and recognition guidance. [CDC Carbon Monoxide Poisoning Basics](https://www.cdc.gov/carbon-monoxide/about/index.html) · [Alternate CDC host](https://portugues.cdc.gov/carbon-monoxide/about/index.html)

### Offline Maps

- Download topographic maps before going offline. [USGS map download guide](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/how-do-i-find-download-or-order-topographic-maps)
- Identify U.S. Topo map symbols. [USGS map symbol guide](https://www.usgs.gov/ngp-standards-and-specifications/us-topo-cartographic-specifications-map-symbol-guide)
- Practice following compass bearings. [National Park Service compass course](https://www.nps.gov/teachers/classrooms/compass-course.htm) · [National Park Service compass lesson plan (PDF)](https://www.nps.gov/buff/learn/education/classrooms/upload/Compass_Course_508.pdf)
- Plan what to do if someone gets lost or injured outdoors. [NPS Outdoor Emergency Plan (PDF)](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/healthandsafety/upload/NPS-Outdoor-Emergency-Plan-508c.pdf)

### Education & Skills

- Study community emergency response. [FEMA CERT Basic Training Participant Manual (PDF)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-HS5_100-PURL-gpo185734/pdf/GOVPUB-HS5_100-PURL-gpo185734.pdf)
- Find hands-on bleeding-control training. [American College of Surgeons training guide](https://www.stopthebleed.org/get-trained/) · [Publisher handouts](https://www.stopthebleed.org/get-trained/posters/)
- Practice making a kit with children. [CDC Ready Wrigley activity booklet (PDF)](https://www.cdc.gov/natural-disasters/media/pdfs/2026/02/RWBuildsEmergency-Kit-Booklet-P.pdf) · [Alternate CDC host (PDF)](https://portugues.cdc.gov/natural-disasters/media/pdfs/2026/02/RWBuildsEmergency-Kit-Booklet-P.pdf)

## Additional references

- **ZIM archives.** The [shared archive catalog](zim-library.md) covers larger offline libraries used across preparedness projects.
- **Wikipedia.** [Selected background articles](../catalog/wikipedia.csv) are grouped by the same eight categories. Use the primary readings above for decisions about health, water, food, or electrical safety.
- **Medical & First Aid.** [Defense Health Agency cold-weather injury guidance](https://www.army.mil/article/282512/cold_weather_injuries_peak_in_january_february) is supplemental reading for cold exposure.
- **Offline Maps.** The Army's [Map Reading and Land Navigation](https://survivalcodex.com/pages/library/fm-3-25-26-land-navigation) is a historical reference for map symbols, grids, scale, and compass skills. Its GPS chapter reflects the 2001 edition.
- **Food and Water.** The [EPA emergency drinking-water disinfection guide](https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/emergency-disinfection-drinking-water) covers a task already represented above. Compare it with the CDC fact sheet during editorial review.
- **Food and Water.** The Army’s [FM 21-10, Field Hygiene and Sanitation (1970)](https://survivalcodex.com/pages/library/fm-21-10-field-hygiene) is a historical reference. The CDC emergency hygiene guide above is the first reading for household use.
- **Survival & Shelter.** [FEMA Earthquake Safety at Home](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-HS5_100-PURL-gpo154686) covers a particular hazard. Lastbook points to a specific checklist page in its water task.
- **Repair.** [iFixit](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/) is a broad collection. In an offline library, link device-specific guides by model from the first screen when available.
