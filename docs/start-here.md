# First readings for an emergency library

This is a browsing draft built from publisher-checked candidates. It covers all eight PrepperCache-aligned categories and uses English-language, primarily U.S. sources. The [resource CSV](../catalog/resources.csv) tracks editions and review status. The [editorial method](editorial-method.md) explains what qualifies for the core tier.

| Category | What do you need to do? | Open the relevant guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medical & First Aid | Assess an emergency, find CPR instructions, or find guidance for severe bleeding | [First Aid Steps](https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/first-aid/performing-first-aid/first-aid-steps), [CPR Steps](https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/cpr/performing-cpr/cpr-steps), [Life-Threatening External Bleeding](https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/learn-first-aid/bleeding-life-threatening-external) |
| Food and Water | Find drinking-water guidance or check food after an outage | [CDC emergency water fact sheet (PDF)](https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/media/pdfs/make-water-safe-during-emergency-p.pdf), [Food Safety During Power Outage](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/food-safety-during-power-outage) |
| Survival & Shelter | Plan for evacuation, sheltering, and household reunification | [Red Cross disaster preparedness plan](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/make-a-plan.html) |
| Repair | Check what to address before starting flood cleanup | [EPA Flooded Homes Cleanup Guidance](https://www.epa.gov/flooded-homes) |
| Communication | Make a printable household contact plan or identify the local alert broadcast | [FEMA Family Emergency Communication Plan (PDF)](https://www.weather.gov/media/afc/Hydro/FloodSafety/family-communication-plan_fillable-card.pdf), [NOAA Weather Radio](https://www.weather.gov/nwr/) |
| Power and Energy | Prepare for an outage or find guidance during one | [CDC power outage guide](https://www.cdc.gov/natural-disasters/response/what-to-do-protect-yourself-during-a-power-outage.html), [Red Cross Power Outage Safety](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/types-of-emergencies/power-outage.html) |
| Offline Maps | Find downloadable topographic maps for your area before going offline | [USGS topographic map download guide](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/how-do-i-find-download-or-order-topographic-maps) |
| Education & Skills | Find organized training material for community emergency response | [FEMA CERT Basic Training Participant Manual (PDF)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-HS5_100-PURL-gpo185734/pdf/GOVPUB-HS5_100-PURL-gpo185734.pdf) |

## Additional references

- **Food and Water.** The [EPA emergency drinking-water disinfection guide](https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/emergency-disinfection-drinking-water) covers a task already represented above. Compare it with the CDC fact sheet during editorial review.
- **Survival & Shelter.** [FEMA Earthquake Safety at Home](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-HS5_100-PURL-gpo154686) covers a particular hazard. Lastbook points to a specific checklist page in its water task.
- **Repair.** [iFixit](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/) is a broad collection. In an offline library, link device-specific guides by model from the first screen when available.
