Lightning strikes the United States about 25 million times a year. Although most lightning occurs in the summer, people can be struck at any time of year. Lightning kills [about 20 people](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-fatalities) in the United States each year, and hundreds more are severely injured. This website will teach you how to [stay safe](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-safety) and offer insight into the [science](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-science) of lightning. You'll find [animated books about lightning](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-outreach), [safety tips](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-safety) for all kinds of situations, [games for kids](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-kids) and [resources for teachers](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-teachers). You'll learn about [lightning victims](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-media) and [survivors](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-survivor). Explore our site, and come and visit often!
